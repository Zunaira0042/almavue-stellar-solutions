SpaceX VR Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Almavue respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SpaceX VR, an immersive educational virtual reality application developed by Almavue, handles information when you use the application on supported Meta Quest devices.

1. Information We Collect

SpaceX VR does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The application does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or services designed to collect personal user information.

2. How Information Is Used

SpaceX VR does not collect personal information for processing, profiling, advertising, or marketing. The application provides its immersive space exploration experience without requiring users to submit personal details.

3. Data Sharing and Storage

Almavue does not receive or maintain personal user information through SpaceX VR. The application does not use an Almavue server or database to store personal user data. Meta and the device operating system may independently process information according to their own policies and settings.

4. Children's Privacy

SpaceX VR does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Users must follow the applicable age requirements and parental supervision settings of the Meta Quest platform.

5. Changes to This Policy

Almavue may update this Privacy Policy if the application's features or information practices change. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Almavue at contact@almavue.com . You can also visit www.almavue.com .
